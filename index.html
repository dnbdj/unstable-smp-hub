Download for iPhone
iOS can’t install unsigned apps directly — Apple requires it, and only a signed app installs on its own. This download works with a free Apple ID using one of these:
Show 2 easy ways to install
  1. AltStore (wireless after setup): install AltStore on a PC, connect your iPhone by USB once, install AltStore with your Apple ID. Then download the .ipa above on your phone and open it with AltStore.
  2. Sideloadly (USB): install Sideloadly on a PC, plug in your iPhone, drag the .ipa in, sign in with your Apple ID, and sideload.
Free Apple IDs work fine. They expire about every 7 days — AltStore re-signs automatically while on Wi-Fi.
